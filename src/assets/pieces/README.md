# Piece set

The "cburnett" chess pieces by [Colin M.L. Burnett](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Cburnett),
from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces/Standard_transparent)
(via the copies lichess serves). The author released them under a choice of the GFDL, the BSD license
and the GPL; EnPawssant uses them under the BSD license.

Changes: the black fill and outlines are recoloured to the app's plum (`#4a3247`) and the fixed
`width`/`height` attributes are removed so the pieces scale with their square.
